      MARK III
      MISSION BRIEFING // FIRST CONTACT

      SUIT UP, TONY

      OBJECTIVE

      Outlast the swarm, chain kills for a score multiplier, and push threat levels. Heavy units arrive every 105 seconds. There is no extraction — only a higher score.

      • CHAIN — kills refresh the combo timer. Higher chain means more score.
      • THREAT — every 45 seconds the arena escalates. Salvage scales with it.
      • ARMOR MARK — your mark is your build. Swap marks in the deck, not mid-run.

      CONTROLS

      • MOVE — left thumb joystick, WASD, or left stick.
      • FIRE — FIRE button, mouse, right trigger; hold to fire.
      • AIM — auto-aim on touch/pad, or drag the right thumb to steer.
      • MISSILES — MSL button, R, or gamepad B.
      • DASH — DASH button, Space, or gamepad A.
      • SWAP — SWAP button, Q/E, or keys 1-5.

      ARSENAL

      • REPULSOR — fast twin palm bolts.
      • UNIBEAM — piercing chest lance.
      • MICRO MISSILES — homing splash.
      • ARC CHAIN — lightning that jumps.
      • PLASMA SCATTER — close-range burst.
      War Machine fights on his own. Keep him alive by clearing the units that swarm him — he rebuilds eight seconds after going down.
